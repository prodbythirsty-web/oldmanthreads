Frequently Asked Questions

☑ Is the bag handmade?+
Yes — every single bag is hand-stitched and hand-braided by one craftsman. No machines, no factories, no shortcuts. Each piece takes hours of careful work and comes out slightly unique.
☑ What can it fit inside?+
These bags are generously sized — wallet, phone, keys, sunglasses, a small notebook, and everyday essentials fit comfortably. Soft yet structured, holds its shape without feeling bulky.
☑ How long does shipping take?+
Orders are prepared within 1–2 business days. Standard worldwide shipping takes 7–12 business days. All orders include tracking.
☑ Can I return or exchange?+
Absolutely. 30-day return policy on all items. If your bag arrives damaged or you’re not satisfied, reach out and we’ll make it right — no awkward questions.
